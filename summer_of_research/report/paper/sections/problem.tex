\section{Problem and Research Question}\label{sec:motivation-and-project-framing}

Regulatory sources such as SIFITO \cite{dgav-sifito} or the European Comissions database of pesticides \cite{ec-pesticides-database} contain information on BCAs and their authorised uses, but finding an option for a given crop and pathogen pair remains difficult.
Different countries will have different authorizations for the same product, and the files containing this information are scattered throughout the web, making them difficult to find and subsequently read.
They also present regulatory or production records rather than answers to a specific crop protection question.
Also, despite the fact that there are some platforms that already show to the users the available BCA products for crop-pathogen treatment, they never show recommendations for other commercial products that may also work in that crop-pathogen.

The research question in this project is: can a metadata-based recommender system identify and rank commercially available biological control products that are potentially suitable for a given crop-pathogen context?
