\section{Problem and Research Question}\label{sec:motivation-and-project-framing}

Regulatory sources such as SIFITO \cite{dgav-sifito} or the European Commission's EU Pesticides Database \cite{ec-pesticides-database} contain information on BCAs and their authorised uses, but finding an option for a given crop and pathogen pair remains difficult.
Different countries may authorise the same product in different ways, and the records are spread across websites with different formats.
They also present regulatory or production records rather than answers to a specific crop protection question.
Some platforms already list BCA products for crop and pest searches, however they do not describe how to rank additional candidates from shared authorisation data.

The research question in this project is: can a recommender system based on record information identify and rank commercially available biological control products that are potentially suitable for a given crop-pathogen context?
